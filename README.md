# Spall-to-Full
Manuscript under submission. The article discusses an algorithm for the depth completion of concrete fire spalling, as well as the measurement of spalling depth or spalling area from arbitrary viewpoints based on a single RGB-D image of a planar curved concrete specimen.

Regarding the measurement of the corresponding algorithms, please refer to the website：https://github.com/MikeyDong/Spall-Depth-and-area-measurement-from-Single-RGBD-image-arbitrary-view-
